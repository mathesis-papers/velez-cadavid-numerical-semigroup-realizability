# Numerical-semigroup realizability and spectral reconstruction

Haobo Ma (ChronoAI Pte Ltd, Singapore) and Wenlin Zhang (National University of Singapore; ChronoAI Pte Ltd, Singapore).

## Results

For a numerical semigroup `T` with minimal positive generators `H`, a positive integer `M` is a possible maximum jump exactly when `M` belongs to `T` and each `h` in `H` satisfies `h<2M` and `2M-h` belongs to `T`.
The unique inclusion-minimal realization is

```
S = {M} union {|M-h| : h in H, h != M}.
```

Its defect semigroup is generated by `M` and all `M-s`, `M+s` for `s` in `S`. Every sufficiently large maximum works. The note also gives an exact threshold for ordinary numerical semigroups and consequences of the cited eventual spectral dimension formulas.

The source is self-contained and has no external bibliography or figures.
Use Tectonic 0.17.0:

```
tectonic note.tex
```

Alternatively run `pdflatex note.tex` twice. The bibliography is included directly in the TeX file.

## Exact finite checks

With Python 3.9 or later and its standard library:

```
python3 verify.py --output reproduced-verification.json
cmp verification.json reproduced-verification.json
```

The default enumerates all 65,536 gap masks on 1 through 16, representing every numerical semigroup with no gap greater than 16, including the full monoid. It compares the prescribed-maximum criterion with exhaustive primitive jump sets through maximum 10 and compares whole semigroups by their least representatives in every residue class. It also checks minimal jump sets, ordinary-semigroup thresholds, and exact inversion of the cited spectral formulas. The latter are arithmetic checks of the formulas, not calculations of graph spectral ranks or stable-range thresholds. Exact counts and the ordered-witness digest are in `verification.json`.

## Mathematical coverage

The complete proofs in `note.tex` establish the prescribed-maximum criterion, the unique smallest jump set, the large-maximum bound, and the ordinary-semigroup threshold. The spectral consequences use the dimension formulas of Vélez Caicedo and Cadavid, arXiv:2611.14316v1, Theorems 1.1–1.3 and Corollaries 3.9–3.10. The formal statement in `D5.S3.Arith.Additive.NumericalSemigroupRealizability.result` is existential realization of each cofinite additive submonoid of the natural numbers by a positive primitive finite jump set, including the full monoid. Its exact source is:

https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/blob/8acf7efa41af7fc9ddbbd39571e7ef73462f4509/D5/S3/Arith/Additive/NumericalSemigroupRealizability.lean

The additional fixed-maximum and spectral statements have written proofs; the finite checker has the ranges described above.
